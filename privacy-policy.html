Privacy Policy — Conquer

Last updated: November 2026  ·  Back to home

Short version: Conquer runs entirely on your machine. Your chess positions, moves, and game data never leave your browser. The extension holds no servers and collects no analytics.

What Conquer collects

The extension stores the following data locally in your browser using Chrome's built-in storage API:

  • Your extension settings (engine depth, auto-move preferences, display options)
  • A cached record of your license status so the extension does not need to verify on every move

This data never leaves your device.

Chess data and analysis

All analysis is performed locally using a WebAssembly build of Stockfish. No board positions, moves, game IDs, or usernames are sent to any external server.

When you use the opening explorer feature, your current board position is sent to explorer.lichess.ovh (Lichess's public opening database API) in standard notation only.

License verification

License keys are verified entirely offline using a cryptographic signature. No network request is made to verify your license after purchase.

When you purchase a license, the transaction is handled by SellAuth and Stripe under their own privacy policies. Conquer's developer receives only your delivery email address.

No analytics or tracking

Conquer contains no analytics, crash reporting, telemetry, or tracking of any kind.

Permissions

  • chess.com — inject the analysis overlay and read board state
  • storage — save your settings and license cache locally
  • offscreen — run the Stockfish engine in a background worker
  • activeTab — identify which tab triggered a keyboard shortcut

Children

Conquer is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be published at this URL with a revised date.
